Scientific Concepts in Civaka Cintamani

Abstract

This study examines the scientific ideas and observations found in the Tamil epic Civaka Cintamani. The work contains references to various aspects of knowledge related to medicine, nature, environment, architecture, astronomy, and everyday life. These descriptions provide valuable insights into the intellectual and cultural context of the period. The study analyses selected passages from Civaka Cintamani to identify scientific concepts and explores their literary and cultural significance. The research highlights the ways in which scientific observations are integrated into Tamil literary traditions.

Keywords

Civaka Cintamani, Tamil Literature, Scientific Concepts, Science in Literature, Medicine, Nature, Astronomy, Tamil Culture
