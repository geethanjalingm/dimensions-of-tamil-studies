Imagery and Symbolism in Sangam Poems

Abstract

This research article examines the use of imagery and symbolism in Sangam poetry, with particular attention to the poems of Sangam poets such as Avvaiyar, Devakulaththar, and Kapilar. Sangam literature serves as an important repository of the historical, social, cultural, and emotional experiences of ancient Tamil society. In these poems, imagery and symbols play a significant role in expressing subtle emotions, relationships, experiences, and ideas through vivid verbal pictures. The study reveals that imagery and symbolism enhance the aesthetic quality of Sangam poetry while also enabling poets to communicate complex human emotions in a concise and evocative manner. Thus, the imaginative techniques employed in Sangam poetry contribute significantly to its enduring literary and cultural value.

Keywords

Sangam Literature, Sangam Poetry, Imagery, Symbolism, Simile, Metaphor, Avvaiyar, Kapilar, Devakulaththar, Tamil Literature
