முனைவர் த. கீதாஞ்சலி

Social Issues of Women in Thilagavathi’s Short Stories

ஆசிரியர்: முனைவர் த. கீதாஞ்சலி

Abstract: Women’s issues have occupied an important place in contemporary Tamil literature, particularly in the short stories of Thilagavathi. Her short stories portray the various social challenges faced by women within the family and society and reveal the impact of patriarchal attitudes on their lives. This article examines the representation of women-related social issues in Thilagavathi’s short stories, with particular focus on the neglect of the girl child, sexual violence, harassment of women, and the problems encountered by working women. It also analyses the ways in which women respond to these challenges and the consequences arising from such social conditions. The discussion highlights gender discrimination, the preference for male children, and socio-economic factors associated with marriage expenses and dowry. Thus, Thilagavathi’s short stories serve as a literary representation of women’s struggles and contribute to creating awareness about gender equality and social change.

Keywords: Thilagavathi, Tamil Short Stories, Women’s Issues, Girl Child Neglect, Gender Discrimination, Patriarchy, Women’s Empowerment, Social Problems

முனைவர் வ. கிருஷ்ணன்

Imagery and Symbolism in Sangam Poems

ஆசிரியர்: முனைவர் வ. கிருஷ்ணன்

பதவி: துறைத்தலைவர், அரசு கலைக்கல்லூரி, உடுமலைப்பேட்டை

Abstract: This research article examines the use of imagery and symbolism in Sangam poetry, with particular attention to the poems of Sangam poets such as Avvaiyar, Devakulaththar, and Kapilar. Sangam literature serves as an important repository of the historical, social, cultural, and emotional experiences of ancient Tamil society. In these poems, imagery and symbols play a significant role in expressing subtle emotions, relationships, experiences, and ideas through vivid verbal pictures. The study reveals that imagery and symbolism enhance the aesthetic quality of Sangam poetry while also enabling poets to communicate complex human emotions in a concise and evocative manner. Thus, the imaginative techniques employed in Sangam poetry contribute significantly to its enduring literary and cultural value.

Keywords: Sangam Literature, Sangam Poetry, Imagery, Symbolism, Simile, Metaphor, Avvaiyar, Kapilar, Devakulaththar, Tamil Literature

க. கதிரவன்

The Sangam Tradition of the Five Tinais

ஆசிரியர்: க. கதிரவன்

பதவி: துணைப்பேராசிரியர், தமிழ்த்துறை (ஆய்வுமையம்), அரசுக்கல்லூரி, சித்தூர், பாலக்காடு 678 104.

Abstract: The five major tinai categories of Tamil akam tradition—Kurinji, Mullai, Marutham, Neithal, and Palai—occupy an important place in Tamil grammatical and literary traditions. However, these five tinai are not arranged in a uniform order across grammatical texts and literary works. This study comparatively examines the different methods of arranging the five tinai found in major Tamil grammatical texts and Sangam and post-Sangam literature. The study considers the arrangements found in works such as Tolkappiyam, Iraiyanar Akapporul, Nambiyagapporul, Virasoliyam, Swaminatham, Thonnul Vilakkam, and Tamilneri Vilakkam, and compares them with the arrangements found in Akananuru, Ainkurunuru, Kalithogai, and selected Patinenkilkanakku works. Since the five tinai can theoretically be arranged in 120 different orders, the variations observed across texts are significant. The study further examines possible factors underlying these arrangements, including the number of poems assigned to each tinai, the distinctive characteristics and aesthetic qualities of the tinai, and the influence of poets and compilers. Particular attention is given to the arrangement of tinai in Kalithogai and the position of Nallanthuvanar’s Neithal poems at the end of the collection. The study also relates this practice to the broader Tamil literary tradition of placing the author’s own work or name at the conclusion of a collection. The findings suggest that the arrangement of tinai was not governed by a single fixed convention but was influenced by the literary, structural, and editorial choices of authors and compilers.

Keywords: திணை, திணைவைப்புமுறை, ஐந்திணை, அகப்பொருள், சங்க இலக்கியம், இலக்கண நூல்கள், கலித்தொகை, அகநானூறு, ஐங்குறுநூறு, தொகுப்புமுறை; Tinai, Arrangement of Tinai, Five Tinai, Akam Tradition, Sangam Literature, Tamil Grammatical Texts, Kalithogai, Akananuru, Ainkurunuru, Compilation Method

அ. வனிதா

Scientific Concepts in Civaka Cintamani

ஆசிரியர்: அ. வனிதா

பதவி: உதவிப்பேராசிரியர், தமிழ்த்துறை, எத்திராஜ் மகளிர் கல்லூரி, சென்னை - 8

Abstract: Science and technology have become integral to the modern world, with numerous technological devices and mechanisms emerging as a result of scientific advancement. However, references to scientific ideas and technological devices can also be identified in ancient Tamil literature. Civaka Cintamani, composed by Tiruttakkatevar, provides significant evidence of such scientific knowledge and technological imagination. The present study examines the scientific information found in Civaka Cintamani, with particular attention to the various mechanical devices described in the work. The text contains references to a wide range of mechanisms associated with warfare, security, transportation, agriculture, and other practical activities. Among these are devices capable of projecting arrows, spears, and stones, mechanical traps, animal-shaped mechanisms, devices producing molten substances, and other defensive and offensive instruments. References to devices such as the lightning conductor, mechanical chariot, and sugarcane-crushing machine further reveal the technological ideas represented in the text. The descriptions of the numerous mechanisms installed on the fortifications of King Saccandan’s palace demonstrate the sophisticated imagination of mechanical technology in the literary context of the period. This study seeks to identify, classify, and interpret these scientific and technological references in order to understand the contribution of Civaka Cintamani to the history of scientific thought in Tamil literature. The study highlights the presence of scientific awareness and technological imagination in medieval Tamil literary traditions.

Keywords: Civaka Cintamani, Scientific Concepts, Tamil Literature, Science and Technology, Mechanical Devices, Ancient Technology, Tiruttakkatevar, Tamil Scientific Thought, Technological Knowledge, Medieval Tamil Literature

முனைவர் ப. இளங்கோ

Kantal Flower in Sangam Literature

ஆசிரியர்: முனைவர் ப. இளங்கோ

பதவி: உதவிப்பேராசிரியர், தமிழாய்வுத்துறை, பி.ஜி.பி கலை அறிவியல் கல்லூரி, நாமக்கல் – 7

Abstract: Sangam literature provides a rich and detailed representation of the natural world and the close relationship between ancient Tamils and their environment. Although considerable research has been undertaken on themes such as love, valour, generosity, fame, and nature in Sangam literature, individual flowers mentioned in these works have received comparatively less attention. The Kantal flower is one such significant element of the Sangam landscape that deserves closer study. The present research examines the references to the Kantal flower in Sangam literature from a literary and scientific perspective. Sangam poets observed the natural environment with remarkable sensitivity and incorporated various plants, flowers, trees, and ecological features into their poetic compositions. The Kantal flower, its appearance, characteristics, habitat, and association with the landscape are explored through relevant Sangam literary references. The study seeks to identify the botanical and ecological information embedded in the poetic descriptions and to understand how such observations contribute to our knowledge of the natural environment of ancient Tamil society. By examining the Kantal flower through both literary interpretation and a scientific perspective, this study highlights the ecological awareness and close observation of nature evident in Sangam poetry. It also demonstrates the value of Sangam literature as a source for understanding the interaction between Tamil literary imagination, traditional ecological knowledge, and the natural world.

Keywords: Kantal Flower, Sangam Literature, Tamil Literature, Natural Environment, Botanical Knowledge, Traditional Ecological Knowledge, Sangam Poetry, Ancient Tamil Society, Flora, Nature Studies
