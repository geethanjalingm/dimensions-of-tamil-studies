Kantal Flower in Sangam Literature

Abstract

The Kantal flower occupies an important place in Sangam literature and is closely associated with the natural landscape and cultural life of ancient Tamil society. Sangam poets frequently employed flowers and other elements of nature as literary symbols to express human emotions, ecological settings, and social experiences. This study examines the representation and significance of the Kantal flower in Sangam literary texts, with particular attention to its botanical, cultural, and literary dimensions. The study highlights the relationship between the Kantal flower, the landscape tradition, and the aesthetic imagination of Sangam poets.

Keywords

Kantal Flower, Sangam Literature, Tamil Literature, Flowers, Landscape, Tinai, Tamil Culture, Nature
